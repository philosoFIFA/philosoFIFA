No one plays with a libero anymore.
Not since Aristotle did.

philosoFIFA is an ongoing art project that places thinkers, artists and cultural theorists into football card format — assigning them positions, stats and clubs based on their style, method and historical context… and, to some extent, intuition.

The project exists on two levels: as an archive and as a presence in the street.

Stats are fictional and poetic, not academic.
It is a game, an essay, and a joke at the same time.

Street Drops

Stickers mainly in Madrid, with some appearing in other parts of Spain and Europe.
